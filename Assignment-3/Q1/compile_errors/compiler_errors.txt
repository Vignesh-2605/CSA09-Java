WHAT THE THREE ERRORS HAVE IN COMMON
================================================================================
All three are detected at COMPILE time, before the program runs once.
Java's checked-exception rules and its top-down catch matching are
enforced by the compiler rather than discovered by a customer at an ATM.
A language without checked exceptions would accept all three files and
fail only when the card reader happened to be missing.
