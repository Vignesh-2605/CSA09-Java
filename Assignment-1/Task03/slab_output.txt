3.  ALLOCATION: HOW break, continue AND return AVOID WORK
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  facility = [3, 2, 4]  (9 slots)

CALL   | VEHICLE      | RESULT         | CELLS READ  | FLOORS SKIPPED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1      | TN01         | [0, 0]         | 1           | -
2      | TN02         | [0, 1]         | 2           | -
3      | TN03         | [0, 2]         | 3           | -
4      | TN04         | [1, 0]         | 4           | 1 known-full
5      | TN05         | [1, 1]         | 2           | 1 known-full
6      | TN06         | [2, 0]         | 3           | 2 known-full
7      | TN07         | [2, 1]         | 2           | 2 known-full
8      | TN08         | [2, 2]         | 3           | 2 known-full
9      | TN09         | [2, 3]         | 4           | 2 known-full
10     | TN10         | FULL, refused  | 4           | 3 known-full
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  return  : ends the search the instant a slot is taken, so a vehicle
            never reads a cell beyond the one it occupies.
  break   : stops re-reading a floor once it is proved full.
  continue: skips such a floor entirely on every later call, which is
            why 'CELLS READ' stays small instead of growing to 9.

  WHEN NO SUITABLE SLOT IS FREE the method returns null, and the caller
  must refuse entry rather than issue a ticket - no ticket is created,
  no slot is written, and the counter is not advanced, so a refused
  vehicle leaves the system in exactly the state it found it.
