J.  EVIDENCE FOR THE DEFENCE  -  WHAT THE CHANGE ACTUALLY COST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REQUIREMENT                                  | NEW CLASSES  | EXISTING CLASSES MODIFIED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
e-bike, flat fee                             | 2            | 0  (EBike + FlatFee)
delivery van, peak pricing                   | 2            | 0  (DeliveryVan + TimeOfDayRate)
visitor one-day pass                         | 1            | 2  (CampusUser, UserCategory)
5 ragged floors                              | 0            | 0  (longer int[] to the constructor)
typed slots so a van fits                    | 0            | 3  (Slot, SlotFactory, SlotGrid)
the PricingRule abstraction                  | 5            | 6  (Vehicle + 5 vehicle classes)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Read the first two rows together with the last.  Once the PricingRule
  abstraction was paid for - and it WAS a real cost, 6 existing classes
  changed - each new vehicle category became two small additions and
  zero modifications.  That is the trade this redesign makes: a one-off
  cost today in exchange for an open-ended series of free extensions.
