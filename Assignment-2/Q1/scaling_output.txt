READING THE 'x prev' COLUMNS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The String + column should sit near 4.0 and the StringBuilder column
  near 2.0.  Two caveats worth stating rather than hiding:

  1. At the smallest sizes the absolute times are only microseconds, so
     timer resolution and JIT effects dominate and the early ratios are
     unreliable.  The large-n rows carry the evidence.
  2. StringBuilder's ratio can exceed 2.0 because its internal array
     doubles as it grows, which copies the buffer a handful of times -
     that is still O(n) amortised, but it is not perfectly smooth.

  The decisive observation is the final column: the ratio between the two
  designs is not constant.  It GROWS with n.  A constant-factor difference
  would keep that column flat; a difference in complexity class makes it
  climb, and that is what distinguishes 'slower' from 'asymptotically
  worse'.
