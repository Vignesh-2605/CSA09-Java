  C five FIFO buckets: Meena Kumar Lata Dev Ravi John Asha Sita

  all three agree: true

  C is interesting: it needs no comparator and no arrival counter at
  all.  FIFO within a bucket preserves arrival order automatically, and
  scanning buckets 1..5 in order enforces severity.  The two rules the
  comparator encoded are expressed by the STRUCTURE instead.

3.  COMPLEXITY AND SPACE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DESIGN                   | admit          | next           | waiting list     | SPACE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A PriorityQueue          | O(log n)       | O(log n)       | O(n log n) sort  | 1 array slot/case
B sorted LinkedList      | O(n)           | O(1)           | O(n), free       | 24 B node/case
C five FIFO buckets      | O(1)           | O(1)*          | O(n), free       | 1 slot + 5 deques

  * C's next() scans at most 5 buckets.  That is O(k) where k is the
    number of severity LEVELS, not the number of patients - and k is
    fixed at 5 by the triage scale.  A constant bound of 5 is O(1).

  C IS THE BEST OF THE THREE ON EVERY OPERATION, and the reason is worth
  stating precisely: it exploits a property of the PROBLEM that the other
  two ignore.  Severity is drawn from a small, fixed, known range.  A
  PriorityQueue is a general-purpose comparison structure that would work
  for any key whatsoever, and pays log n for that generality.  When the
  key range is bounded, the comparisons can be replaced by indexing -
  the same insight that makes counting sort beat comparison sorts on
  small integer keys.

  WHERE C WOULD STOP BEING THE RIGHT ANSWER: if severity became a
  continuous clinical score rather than 5 discrete levels, the bucket
  array would need one entry per distinct score and the approach would
  collapse.  A PriorityQueue does not care how many distinct keys exist.

  This finding is the starting point for Q7.
