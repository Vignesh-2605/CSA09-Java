3.  WHY A DAMAGED LINE DOES NOT STOP THE SEARCH
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Because the record boundary is the NEWLINE, and the newline is intact
  even when the fields between two newlines are not.  BufferedReader
  finds the next record without understanding the damaged one, so the
  parse failure is contained to a single line.

  Test N7 is the evidence: P1006 is found even though three damaged
  lines sit between it and the start of the file.  That property is a
  consequence of the FORMAT, not of the parsing code - a fixed-length
  binary file has no such self-synchronising boundary, and one short
  write there misaligns every record that follows it.
