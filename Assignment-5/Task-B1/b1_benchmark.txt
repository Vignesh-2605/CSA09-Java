3.  PASS SEARCH AS THE FILE GROWS FROM 1,000 TO 100,000 RECORDS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  RECORDS      | FIRST (ms)       | MIDDLE (ms)      | LAST (ms)        | MISSING (ms)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1,000        | 0.06             | 3.90             | 4.80             | 2.17
  10,000       | 0.03             | 6.98             | 13.77            | 15.33
  50,000       | 0.02             | 24.33            | 35.74            | 31.60
  100,000      | 0.02             | 20.50            | 34.57            | 47.69

  The FIRST column barely moves: find() stops at the first match, so a
  hit at the top of the file costs the same however big the file is.
  The LAST and MISSING columns grow with the file, because both read
  every line.  That is O(n) per search, and the missing case is the
  worst case every time.
