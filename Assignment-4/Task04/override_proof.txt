CONCLUSION
MouseAdapter is a CLASS with empty implementations, not an interface with
abstract methods, so an anonymous subclass is free to declare any method
it likes.  mouseClick(MouseEvent) is a perfectly legal new method that
simply overrides nothing, so nothing ever calls it.  @Override asks the
compiler to assert that the method DOES override something, and the
assertion fails at compile time - before the program is ever run, and
long before a user wonders why clicking does nothing.
